# Circles And Distances

## Problem Description
Given the coordinates of centers and radii of two circles in a 2D plane, determine whether the circles intersect, touch, are disjoint, or one is contained in the other, and compute the shortest distance between their circumferences.

---

## Constraints
- Standard TCS CodeVita limits.
- Time Limit: 1.0 - 2.0 seconds
- Memory Limit: 256 MB

---

## Input Format
- X1, Y1, R1 (Circle 1)
- X2, Y2, R2 (Circle 2)

---

## Output Format
- Print the relationship status and the shortest distance between circumferences with 2 decimal places.
